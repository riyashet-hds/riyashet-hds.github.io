About Me

01 / Background

I'm Riya Shet, a data scientist based in Dubai. I recently finished an MSc in Health Data Science at the University of Birmingham Dubai, after a First Class BSc in psychology, where I was trained in experimental design and statistics.

Before the MSc, I was a research associate at a psychiatry clinic. I built a KPI-based investment case that turned clinic operations into service-efficiency targets for a new pilot. That work taught me that a strong idea needs the right systems around it before anyone can rely on it.

02 / Research

My dissertation, supervised by Dr Le Zhang, became my first paper. It was accepted at the BraTS-GoAT workshop at MICCAI 2026.

I trained deep-learning models to segment brain tumours on MRI, then simulated the scanner and protocol changes real hospitals introduce. Accuracy fell while the model's own confidence barely moved. Disagreement between several models caught the shift that a single model's confidence missed.

The full pipeline is public and reproducible, and I packaged the model in Docker for the challenge's external evaluation (whole-tumour Dice 0.87 on the official validation leaderboard). Paper · Code

03 / How I Work

I design experiments to catch my own mistakes before anyone else does.

  • I test models under the conditions they will meet. In my retinopathy audit, a strong headline score (kappa 0.88) hid only 48.6% sensitivity on the most urgent grade.
  • I report results that don't flatter me. In a colorectal cancer study, two extra data sources added just 0.003 AUC, so I reported no meaningful gain.
  • I make work reproducible. Fixed seeds, repeated cross-validation, committed SQL queries and containerised models.
  • I tie models to decisions. I simulated 10,000 scenarios to test whether a hospital should fund an AI triage tool.
